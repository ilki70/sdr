# Market Reference Cases

Updated: 2026-03-06

## Why this matters
These public references were selected to anchor product decisions for Super Vendedor around three ideas:
- AI agents need to prove operational value, not just produce fluent replies.
- The strongest outcomes appear when the agent is connected to knowledge, routing, and next-step workflows.
- Demo experience matters because trust improves after people see the agent working in context.

## Reference cases

### HubSpot Breeze Customer Agent traction
Source: https://ir.hubspot.com/news-releases/news-release-details/hubspot-credits/

What matters:
- HubSpot stated on 2025-05-08 that for thousands of customers, Breeze Customer Agent is resolving more than 50% of support conversations, and in some cases 80%.
- They also positioned the same agent beyond support, including marketing and sales inquiries.

Implication for Super Vendedor:
- Our product should show measurable automation rate and assisted-conversion rate.
- The roadmap should keep the agent usable across the full buyer journey, not only inside a support-like chat box.

### Sandler: more SQLs passed to sales
Source: https://www.hubspot.com/spotlight/fall2024

What matters:
- HubSpot attributes to Sandler a 60% increase in SQLs passed from marketing to sales that convert into potential pipeline.

Implication for Super Vendedor:
- We need explicit metrics around lead qualification quality, not only reply volume.
- The dashboard should expose handoff quality and conversion-to-pipeline indicators.

### Kaplan: inquiry deflection plus faster response
Source: https://www.hubspot.com/case-studies/kaplan
Source: https://www.hubspot.com/drive-retention-for-education

What matters:
- Kaplan reduced response times by 30%.
- 25% to 30% of customer inquiries were resolved without human intervention.

Implication for Super Vendedor:
- Demo and landing should communicate that AI value is speed plus consistency, not just labor reduction.
- Public proof blocks should highlight response time, qualification speed, and human handoff savings.

### Transkribus: 60% of inquiries resolved automatically
Source: https://community.hubspot.com/t5/Breeze-Library/3-Game-Changing-Breeze-Use-Cases-that-Delivered-Measurable-ROI/ba-p/1127761
Source: https://blog.hubspot.com/marketing/breeze-future-of-gtm

What matters:
- HubSpot cites Transkribus resolving 60% of customer inquiries automatically.

Implication for Super Vendedor:
- The knowledge layer and playbook quality are core product levers.
- We should keep investing in simulation/evaluation tooling because automation rate depends on grounded answers.

### Intercom: seeing the agent builds trust
Source: https://www.intercom.com/campaign/ai-end-user-sentiment-report

What matters:
- Intercom reported in a 2025 study that positivity toward AI in support increased by 20 percentage points after users saw a modern AI agent in action.
- Trust in AI agents to resolve issues rose by 18 percentage points after the demo.

Implication for Super Vendedor:
- The interactive public demo is not decorative; it is part of conversion.
- We should continue investing in a strong visual + live demo experience for the landing.

### Intercom: sales bots that qualify leads
Source: https://www.intercom.com/resources/guides/sales-chatbots
Source: https://www.intercom.com/help/en/articles/12508017-fin-as-a-customer-agent

What matters:
- Intercom keeps emphasizing automatic lead qualification and use of AI across sales, success, and marketing.

Implication for Super Vendedor:
- Qualification state, next-step suggestion, and handoff readiness should remain first-class entities in the product.

## Product direction pulled from these references
1. Public demo should feel real and measurable.
2. Dashboard should show qualification and handoff metrics, not vanity chat metrics.
3. The agent should operate with grounded knowledge and explicit next-step logic.
4. Human handoff should be visible and structured.
5. Landing should sell the business outcome: faster qualification, better conversion, better pipeline quality.
